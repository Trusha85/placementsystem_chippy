## 1. Purpose and scope
1.1 These guidelines govern campus recruitment of NFSU students in: M.Tech. CS, M.Tech. AIDS, B.Tech.-M.Tech. CSE, M.Sc. CS and M.Sc. DFIS.
1.2 The Placement Cell is the competent authority for exceptions and disciplinary decisions. The software only flags and explains.

## 2. Definitions
- **I (Internship only):** internship with no assured full-time role. **F (FTE only):** full-time employment. **I+F (Internship + PPO/FTE):** internship carrying a pre-placement offer or FTE conversion.
- **CTC:** annual fixed compensation in LPA (INR lakh per annum). **Stipend:** internship payment, reported separately from CTC.
- **Active offer:** an offer whose acceptance status is *Pending* or *Accepted*. Declined and expired offers impose no restriction.
- **Placed student:** a registered student with at least one active offer. A student with several offers is counted once.

## 3. Registration and eligibility
3.1 Only students with *Registered* placement status may participate. Deregistered or non-registered students may not sit any drive.
3.2 A student must be *Eligible*. *Pending verification* is referred to the Placement Cell before participation. Ineligible students may not participate.
3.3 Company-specific criteria are checked before participation. **[A]** Programme is the criterion recorded in the system.

## 4. Company categories (by FTE package)
| Category | Package (CTC) |
|---|---|
| A+ | 16 LPA and above |
| A | 10 LPA to below 16 LPA |
| B | 6 LPA to below 10 LPA |
| C | Below 6 LPA |

4.1 Category is derived from the FTE package. **[A]** Internship-only offers use the category assigned to the company by the Placement Cell.

## 5. Offer types and restrictions
5.1 **Internship only (I):** after selection, the student cannot apply for another internship-only role. FTE and I+F roles remain open, subject to §6.
5.2 **FTE only (F):** after selection, the student cannot apply for another FTE-only role, but may apply for internship roles. **[A]** A further FTE or I+F role is permitted only as a category switch under §6.
5.3 **Internship + PPO/FTE (I+F):** after selection, the student cannot participate in any other I, F or I+F role at another organisation.

## 6. Switching rules
6.1 **A+:** no switching. A student selected in an A+ company with an FTE component is out of the placement process.
6.2 **A:** switching allowed only to A+ companies.
6.3 **B:** a further placement is allowed only if the new package is at least **1.5 times** the existing package. If the new package is not yet known, the case goes to manual review.
6.4 **C:** eligible only for higher-category opportunities (B, A or A+).
6.5 Declining an offer or letting it expire releases the student from restrictions linked to that offer.

## 7. Attendance and conduct
7.1 Attendance is recorded at pre-placement talks, presentations, tests, interviews and other recruitment activities.
7.2 An unexcused absence flags the student for possible exclusion from the **next three drives**. Emergency exceptions (for example medical) are recorded as "excused" by the Placement Cell. The system refers flagged cases for review; it does not exclude anyone automatically.

## 8. Offer acceptance and joining
8.1 The student must accept or decline within **24 hours of the offer or the company's stated timeline, whichever is earlier**. Offers not answered in time are marked *Expired*. Only the Placement Administrator may reinstate an expired offer.
8.2 Joining status (*Pending*, *Joined*, *Not joined*) and PPO status (*Pending*, *Offered*, *Accepted*, *Declined*) are updated by the Placement Cell.

## 9. Off-campus offers, deregistration and NOC
9.1 Off-campus offers must be reported and are recorded in the student's history. If an off-campus offer would breach §5 or §6 it is recorded and flagged for review.
9.2 A student who deregisters stops being eligible for further drives. Existing offers and history are retained.
9.3 Where a No-Objection Certificate (NOC) is required, its status (*Pending*, *Issued*, *Denied*) is recorded against the offer.

## 10. Violations and exceptions
10.1 Suspected violations are flagged for review. The Placement Cell/competent authority takes the final decision and may approve an exception. In the system only the Placement Administrator can override a block, and must record a written justification.
10.2 Placement history is never overwritten: corrections create audit entries showing who changed what and when.

## 11. Data protection
11.1 Mobile numbers and compensation are visible only to roles that need them (Administrator, Officer; a student sees their own record). Coordinators see their programme without contact or compensation details. Recruiters see only their own company's offers.
11.2 Exports containing personal or salary data are restricted to the Placement Administrator and are logged.
11.3 **[A]** Student records are retained for the session plus 3 years, then archived or anonymised on the Administrator's instruction.

## 12. Review
12.1 These guidelines are reviewed at the end of each placement session by the Placement Cell.
